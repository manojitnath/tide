# Focus MN illustrations: prompts

Three pictures of the same character for the panel: **Ready**, **Deep work** and **Break**.
Generate the first one, pick the character you like, then attach that image to the next two prompts
("use this exact character") so all three match.

## Shared style (paste at the start of every prompt)

> Minimal editorial illustration, black ink line art with a slightly hand-drawn wobble, bold confident outlines, flat solid black fills for hair and some clothing, light grey halftone dot shading on a few areas only, no other colours. Simple rounded cartoon character with a small head, dot or short-line eyes, tiny nose, calm expression, chunky simplified hands. Lots of empty space around the figure. Plain off-white paper background (#F6F4EE), no frame, no border, no box, no scenery, no text, no shadows except a faint flat ellipse on the ground. Full figure, centred, square image. Style of modern Notion / Headspace-like spot illustrations.

## 1. Ready

## 2. Deep work

## 3. Break

> [shared style] The same character lying on their back on the floor, knees up, one leg crossed over the other with the foot raised, hands behind the head, eyes closed and smiling, a small doodled star and a curly squiggle floating above them.

## Tips

- **ChatGPT:** generate *Ready* first. Then in the same chat: "Now draw this exact same character, same style and proportions: [Deep work prompt]".
- **Nano Banana (Gemini):** upload the *Ready* result as a reference image with each later prompt. It is good at keeping a character consistent.
- If the background comes out white or textured, ask for "a transparent background, PNG", or I can remove it.
- Ask for the largest size (1024 × 1024 or more). Send me the PNGs and I can trace them to SVG, remove the background, and add small motions (steam, foot swing, typing) if the parts are clear.
